## BEFORE FIRST USE

Remove all packaging materials and promotional stickers from the appliance. Wash the basket and pan in warm soapy water, rinse thoroughly, and dry completely. Wipe the exterior of the main unit with a damp cloth. Do not immerse the main unit in water.

Before cooking food, run the appliance empty for 10 minutes at 400°F to eliminate any manufacturing residue. This is normal and any smoke or odor should dissipate quickly. Ensure adequate ventilation during this process.

---

## OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS

**Step 1:** Place the appliance on a stable, level, heat-resistant surface. Ensure at least 5 inches of clearance on all sides for proper air circulation.

**Step 2:** Pull the basket out of the appliance by the handle. Place food in the basket. Do not overfill. For best results, arrange food in a single layer with space between pieces.

**Step 3:** Slide the basket back into the appliance until you hear a click.

**Step 4:** Plug the power cord into a grounded electrical outlet.

**Step 5:** Press the power button to turn on the appliance. The display will illuminate.

**Step 6:** Select a pre-set program by pressing the corresponding icon, or manually set temperature and time using the plus and minus buttons.

**Step 7:** Press the start button to begin cooking. The appliance will beep and begin heating.

**Step 8:** During cooking, you may pull out the basket to check food progress. The appliance will automatically pause. Return the basket to resume cooking.

**Step 9:** When the timer reaches zero, the appliance will beep five times and automatically shut off.

**Step 10:** Pull out the basket and place it on a heat-resistant surface. Use tongs or a spatula to remove food. Never turn the basket upside down while it is still attached to the pan, as excess oil will leak onto food.

## COOKING TIPS

For crispier results, lightly spray or brush food with oil before cooking. Shake or turn foods halfway through cooking time for even browning. When cooking fresh vegetables, add one to two tablespoons of oil for better texture.

Frozen foods typically require two to three minutes longer than fresh foods at the same temperature. Do not overcrowd the basket, as this will prevent proper air circulation and result in unevenly cooked food.

Smaller batches generally produce crispier results. Foods with high moisture content may require slightly longer cooking times. Preheating the appliance for three minutes before adding food can improve results for certain recipes.

Items that cook well together can be prepared simultaneously, but ensure they require similar temperatures and cooking times. Use baking parchment or perforated parchment paper to prevent delicate foods from sticking.

---

## CLEANING AND MAINTENANCE

**IMPORTANT:** Always unplug the appliance and allow it to cool completely before cleaning.

**After Each Use:** Remove the basket and pan. Wash in warm soapy water or place in the dishwasher. For stubborn residue, soak in warm water for 10 minutes before scrubbing with a non-abrasive sponge.

Do not use metal utensils or abrasive cleaners, as they will damage the non-stick coating. The main unit should be wiped clean with a damp cloth only. Never immerse the main unit in water.

The heating element can be cleaned by wiping gently with a soft brush after the unit has cooled. Ensure the interior is completely dry before using again.

**Regular Maintenance:** Inspect the power cord regularly for damage. Check that the basket and pan are properly seated before each use. Ensure air intake and exhaust vents are free from dust and debris.

## TROUBLESHOOTING

**Problem:** Appliance does not turn on.  
**Solution:** Check that the power cord is properly plugged into a functioning outlet. Ensure the basket is fully inserted. Press the power button firmly.

**Problem:** Food is not cooked evenly.  
**Solution:** Shake or turn food halfway through cooking. Avoid overcrowding the basket. Ensure food pieces are similar in size.

**Problem:** Food is not crispy.  
**Solution:** Use less food for crispier results. Add a small amount of oil to food before cooking. Increase temperature by 10-20 degrees.

**Problem:** White smoke coming from appliance.  
**Solution:** You may be cooking high-fat foods. Excess grease in the pan can cause smoking. This is normal. Ensure the pan is cleaned thoroughly after each use.

**Problem:** Fresh fries cook unevenly.  
**Solution:** Soak cut potatoes in cold water for 30 minutes to remove excess starch. Pat dry thoroughly before cooking. Use similar-sized pieces.

**Problem:** Display shows error code E1.  
**Solution:** Temperature sensor malfunction. Unplug appliance and allow to cool for 30 minutes. If error persists, contact customer service.

**Problem:** Appliance emits unusual odor.  
**Solution:** During first uses, some odor is normal as manufacturing residue burns off. If odor persists after several uses, ensure all packaging materials have been removed and the unit is clean.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I cook frozen foods without defrosting?**  
A: Yes, the TurboFlow 3000 can cook most frozen foods directly. Add two to three minutes to the recommended cooking time and check for doneness before serving.

**Q: Do I need to preheat the air fryer?**  
A: Preheating is not required but is recommended for certain foods like steaks or baked goods. Preheat for three minutes at your desired temperature before adding food.

**Q: How much oil should I use?**  
A: Most foods require little to no oil. For fresh vegetables or homemade fries, one to two tablespoons is sufficient. Frozen foods typically have enough oil already.

**Q: Can I use aluminum foil or parchment paper?**  
A: Yes, you may use both. Ensure foil or paper does not block air flow and is properly secured so it does not touch the heating element. Use perforated parchment designed for air fryers for best results.

**Q: Why does my food stick to the basket?**  
A: Lightly spray the basket with cooking oil before adding food. Ensure food has a light coating of oil. Some delicate items like fish benefit from parchment paper.

**Q: Can I stack food in the basket?**  
A: For best results, arrange food in a single layer. Stacking is possible but will require longer cooking time and food should be shaken or turned more frequently.

**Q: Is the basket coating safe?**  
A: Yes, the basket uses PFOA-free non-stick coating that meets all safety standards. Do not use metal utensils that could scratch the surface.

**Q: How do I adjust recipes from a traditional oven?**  
A: Reduce the temperature by 25 degrees Fahrenheit and reduce cooking time by approximately 20%. Monitor food closely during first attempts and adjust as needed.

**Q: Can I cook multiple types of food at once?**  
A: Yes, if they require similar temperatures and cooking times. Be aware that flavors may transfer between foods. Use dividers or foil to separate items if desired.

**Q: Why does the appliance stop when I pull out the basket?**  
A: This is a safety feature. Cooking automatically pauses when the basket is removed and resumes when reinserted. This allows you to check or shake food safely.

**Q: How long does the air fryer take to cool down?**  
A: Allow approximately 30 minutes for the appliance to cool completely after use before cleaning or storing.

**Q: Can I wash the basket in the dishwasher?**  
A: Yes, both the basket and pan are dishwasher safe. Place on the top rack for best results.
